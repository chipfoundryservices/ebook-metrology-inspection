## C. Comparison: Optical vs. E-Beam

| Metric | Optical AOI | E-Beam SEM |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | 100-150 nm | 5-10 nm |
| Throughput | 1000+ w/month | 100-300 w/month |
| Cost (equipment) | $0.8-1.2M | $2.5-4M |
| Cost (service/year) | $100-150K | $200-300K |
| Precision | 92-95% | 97-99% |
| Complexity | Low | High |
| Customer lock-in | 5-7 years | 7-10 years |
| Margin (equipment) | 50-55% | 50-55% |
| Margin (service) | 70-75% | 70-75% |
