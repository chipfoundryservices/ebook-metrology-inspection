## Cross-References to Book 7 Concepts

### Optical Inspection Limits

- **Related:** Book 3 (Lithography Ch.2) - diffraction limit discussion
- **Parallel:** Both optical lithography and optical inspection hit λ/2NA = 100nm limit
- **Solution:** Both moving to new wavelengths (EUV for litho, E-beam for metrology)

## Investment Thesis Summary

**Why metrology is undervalued:**

1. **Physics moat** (e-beam wavelength advantage) is durable 10+ years
2. **ML moat** (dataset size) is unique to large-installed-base vendors
3. **Process control moat** (APC recipes) is worth $50-100M per fab
4. **ROI** (4-8 month payback) highest of any fab capex
5. **Service revenue** (70-75% margin) compounds for 7-10 years per tool

**Expected vendor ROIC: 25-35% through 2030** (vs. foundries 15-25%)
