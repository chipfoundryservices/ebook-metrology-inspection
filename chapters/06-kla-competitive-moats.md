## I. KLA's Moat: Why Dominance Is Durable

### Layer 1: Installed Base (3,000+ tools globally)

Each tool generates $500K-$1M annual service revenue (consumables, software, maintenance).

**Installed base value:** 3,000 tools × $700K/year = $2.1B annual service revenue

This is a **recurring revenue stream** independent of new equipment sales. It compounds without additional capex.

### Layer 2: ML Dataset

15+ years of defect images from 1,000+ fabs = **billions of labeled defects**

This dataset trains the best CNNs in the industry. Competitors would need 5-10 years to build comparable datasets.

### Layer 3: Process Control IP

Fabs develop proprietary APC recipes for KLA equipment. Switching to AMAT or Onto Innovation costs $50-100M+ in lost recipes and rework.

### Layer 4: Customer Relationships

KLA has deep relationships with process engineers at TSMC, Samsung, Intel. Equipment selection driven by engineering confidence, not price.

---

## II. Why Competitors Struggle

**AMAT:** Strong in e-beam but weak in optical. Cannot match KLA's full-stack solution.

**Onto Innovation:** Acquired optical metrology companies but lacks e-beam leadership. Slow to catch up.

**Regional players:** Cannot achieve scale to build competitive datasets.

---

## III. ROIC Analysis

**KLA metrology division (estimated):**
- Revenue: $4-5B annually
- Gross margin: 52-55% (equipment) + 72-75% (service) = blended 60%+
- ROIC: 32-38% (high, but lower than deposition 38-42%)

Why lower? E-beam is slower, APC complexity drives up support costs. But ROIC still exceptional.
