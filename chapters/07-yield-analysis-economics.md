# Chapter 7: Yield Analysis & Economics

## The Yield Economy

**Simple truth:** 1% yield improvement is worth $30-50M annually to a 100K wafers/month fab.

Yield is therefore the **most valuable metric in semiconductor manufacturing.** Metrology is the tool that achieves yield improvement.

---

## I. Yield Learning Curves

**New node ramp-up:** Yield starts at 20%, improves to 95%+ over 18-24 months

```
Months 1-3:   Yield 20-30% (early learning)
Months 4-6:   Yield 40-50% (recipe optimization)
Months 7-12:  Yield 60-80% (process control tightening)
Months 13-24: Yield 90-95%+ (mature manufacturing)
```

**Key driver of learning rate:** Metrology feedback quality

Fab with superior defect detection (KLA equipment) achieves 95% yield in 18 months.
Fab with inferior defect detection (competitor equipment) achieves 95% yield in 24+ months.

**6-month advantage = $500M+ in early revenue.**

---

## II. Yield Drivers: Defect Density

**Parametric yield:** Wafer meets electrical specs (voltage, current, power)

**Defects yield:** Wafer meets defect-free manufacturing (no opens, shorts, particles)

At 3nm nodes, **defects yield dominates.** A single undetected void kills the die.

**Defect density targets:**

| Node | Target defects/cm² | Detection requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 28nm | 0.1 | Optical AOI sufficient |
| 7nm | 0.01 | Optical AOI + selective E-beam |
| 3nm | 0.001 | E-beam SEM mandatory |

---

## III. Fab Economics: Yield Impact

**Scenario:** 50K wafers/month fab, 100mm² die, 400 dies per wafer

Monthly production: 50K × 400 = 20M dies

**At 90% yield:** 18M good dies/month

**At 95% yield:** 19M good dies/month

**Difference:** 1M additional good dies/month × $5-20/die (wholesale) = **$5-20M additional monthly revenue**

---

## IV. ROIC of Metrology Investment

**Fab capex for metrology equipment:** $15-20M (8-12 tools)

**Annual yield improvement from metrology:** $30-50M

**Payback period:** 4-8 months

This is why metrology equipment ROI is **higher than any other fab investment** (lithography, etch, deposition).
